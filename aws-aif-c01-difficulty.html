Is the AWS AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) exam hard?

Short answer: no — it's a foundational exam. But it's broad, and people who skim it lose points on service selection and responsible-AI questions. Here's what it tests and how to know you're ready.

By ExamFastTrack Updated 2026 7 min read

The AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) is AWS's entry-level AI certification. It's aimed at people who work with AI solutions — product managers, analysts, consultants, developers new to ML — rather than people who build models from scratch.

The honest difficulty rating

Easy to moderate. There's no coding and no maths derivations. The challenge is breadth: ML fundamentals, generative AI concepts, a long list of AWS services, and responsible AI all appear. Most failures come from confusing similar services, not from hard concepts.

The exam at a glance

  • Questions: 65 (50 scored, 15 unscored).
  • Time: 90 minutes.
  • Passing score: 700 / 1000.
  • Question types: multiple choice, multiple response, ordering, matching and case studies.
  • Cost: $100, at a Pearson VUE test centre or online proctored.
  • Prerequisite: none.

What the exam covers

  • Fundamentals of AI and ML (20%) — supervised vs. unsupervised learning, training vs. inference, the ML lifecycle.
  • Fundamentals of generative AI (24%) — foundation models, tokens, embeddings, hallucinations, cost and latency trade-offs.
  • Applications of foundation models (28%) — the biggest domain: prompt engineering, RAG, fine-tuning vs. in-context learning, evaluation, and Amazon Bedrock.
  • Guidelines for responsible AI (14%) — bias, fairness, explainability, transparency.
  • Security, compliance and governance (14%) — IAM, encryption, data privacy and governance for AI workloads.

What actually trips people up

Picking the right service

Bedrock vs. SageMaker vs. the pre-built AI services (Comprehend, Rekognition, Textract, Transcribe, Translate, Polly, Lex, Kendra). The exam loves scenarios where the "powerful" option is wrong because a simpler managed service fits the requirement.

RAG vs. fine-tuning vs. prompt engineering

Know which one to reach for given cost, freshness of data and effort. Questions often hinge on "the lowest-effort option that meets the requirement."

Responsible AI tooling

SageMaker Clarify, Guardrails for Amazon Bedrock and model cards show up more than beginners expect.

Rule of thumb: when two answers both work, AWS usually wants the more managed, lower-effort one — unless the question explicitly demands custom control.

How long to study

Two to three weeks for most people with some cloud or IT background. Read the exam guide, learn each service's one-sentence purpose, then move to timed practice exams.

Common questions

Is AIF-C01 hard?

It's foundational — no coding. The difficulty is breadth: many services and concepts, with similar-sounding options.

What's the passing score?

700 out of 1000, across 65 questions (50 scored) in 90 minutes.

Do I need to code?

No. You need to understand concepts and pick the right AWS service for a use case.

Does it expire?

Yes — AWS certifications are valid for three years.
