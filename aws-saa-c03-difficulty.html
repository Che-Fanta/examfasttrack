AWS · Exam difficulty

Is the AWS Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03) exam hard?

Short answer: yes, it's a real step up from the foundational exams. Long scenario questions, many services, and two answers that both work — you have to pick the best one.

By ExamFastTrack Updated 2026 8 min read

AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) is AWS's most popular certification. It tests whether you can design secure, resilient, performant and cost-effective architectures on AWS.

The honest difficulty rating

Moderate to hard. Harder than Cloud Practitioner or AI Practitioner, easier than the Professional level. Hands-on experience makes a big difference: many questions describe a scenario in several sentences and expect you to spot the one requirement that decides the answer.

The exam at a glance

  • Questions: 65 (50 scored, 15 unscored).
  • Time: 130 minutes.
  • Passing score: 720 / 1000.
  • Question types: multiple choice and multiple response.
  • Cost: $150, test centre or online proctored.
  • Validity: three years.

What the exam covers

  • Design secure architectures (30%) — IAM, KMS, VPC security, data protection.
  • Design resilient architectures (26%) — Multi-AZ, decoupling with SQS/SNS, disaster recovery.
  • Design high-performing architectures (24%) — storage, compute, database and caching choices.
  • Design cost-optimized architectures (20%) — pricing models, storage classes, right-sizing.

What actually trips people up

Storage choices

S3 storage classes and lifecycle rules, EBS volume types, EFS vs. FSx. Small details — retrieval time, minimum storage duration — decide the answer.

Databases

RDS Multi-AZ vs. read replicas, Aurora, DynamoDB with DAX, ElastiCache. Know which one solves availability and which one solves read performance.

Networking

VPC endpoints, NAT gateways, Transit Gateway, security groups vs. NACLs, Route 53 routing policies.

"Most cost-effective" and "least operational overhead"

These phrases change the right answer. Train yourself to underline them.

Rule of thumb: read the last sentence of the question first — it tells you what's being optimised.

How long to study

Six to ten weeks with some AWS experience; longer if you're new to cloud. Combine a course with hands-on labs, then finish with timed practice exams.

How to know you're ready

Common questions

Is SAA-C03 hard?

It's a real step up from the foundational exams: long scenarios and several plausible answers.

Do I need Cloud Practitioner first?

No. It's optional, though it helps if you're new to AWS.

Is there a lab section?

No. All questions are multiple choice or multiple response.

Does it expire?

Yes, after three years.
