What these free questions cover

6 exam-style sample questions, each with a full explanation of why the right answer is right and why the others are wrong:

  1. Mid-Sprint, a senior stakeholder asks a Developer directly to add a new feature. What is the most appropriate Scrum Master response?
  2. The Developers consistently finish Sprint work with days to spare and then sit idle. What should the Scrum Master do first?
  3. Management asks the Scrum Master to commit the team to a fixed scope and a fixed deadline. What is the best response?
  4. The Developers realize mid-Sprint that they cannot complete all forecasted work. Who should review and adjust the scope of work within the Sprint?
  5. A key stakeholder and the Product Owner both raise concerns about slow system performance that has not been addressed in recent Sprints. What is the most appropriate action for the Scrum Master?
  6. The Developers propose removing performance testing from the Definition of Done in order to meet an upcoming deadline. What is the best course of action for the Scrum Master?
