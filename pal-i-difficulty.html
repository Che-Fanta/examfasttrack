Is the PAL I exam hard?

Short answer: fewer questions and more time than PSM I — but the answers are about mindset, not rules, which makes them harder to memorise.

By ExamFastTrack Updated 2026 6 min read

Professional Agile Leadership I (PAL I) from Scrum.org is aimed at managers and leaders who support agile teams. It tests whether you understand how leadership behaviour enables — or blocks — agility.

The honest difficulty rating

Easy to moderate. The time pressure is low. The difficulty is that there's no single short document to memorise like the Scrum Guide: questions are about leadership choices, and several options often sound reasonable. Traditional command-and-control instincts lead straight to wrong answers.

The exam at a glance

  • Questions: 36.
  • Time: 60 minutes — well over a minute and a half per question.
  • Passing score: 85% (31 correct).
  • Question types: multiple choice, multiple answer.
  • Cost: $200 per attempt, online.
  • Validity: lifetime.

What actually trips people up

Managing vs. enabling

The right answer rarely involves a leader assigning work, setting team targets or approving decisions. It involves clarifying goals, removing organisational impediments and giving teams autonomy.

Measuring agility

Expect questions on Evidence-Based Management: current value, unrealised value, ability to innovate and time to market — not utilisation or velocity.

Organisational change

Scaling, structures and culture: why reorganising around value streams beats adding coordination layers.

Few questions, high stakes

With only 36 questions, each mistake costs almost 3%. Six wrong answers and you fail.

Rule of thumb: pick the answer that increases transparency and team autonomy, not the one that increases control.

How long to study

One to two weeks. Read the Scrum Guide and the Evidence-Based Management Guide, then work through practice questions and the reasoning behind each answer.

Common questions

Is PAL I hard?

Not very, but it's easy to underestimate. Six mistakes mean failing.

Who should take PAL I?

Managers, leaders and coaches who support agile teams.

Do I need PSM I first?

No, but Scrum knowledge helps a lot.

Does PAL I expire?

No. It's a lifetime certification.
