Is the PSPO I exam hard?

Short answer: about as hard as PSM I, with a different twist. You need the Scrum Guide plus a product-and-value mindset — and 85% to pass.

By ExamFastTrack Updated 2026 7 min read

Professional Scrum Product Owner I (PSPO I) from Scrum.org validates that you understand the Product Owner accountability in Scrum: maximising value, managing the Product Backlog and working with stakeholders.

The honest difficulty rating

Moderate. Everything in the Scrum Guide is fair game, plus product-management thinking that goes slightly beyond it: value, outcomes vs. output, empiricism in product decisions. People who act as "requirements managers" or proxy POs in their job often find the exam harder than expected.

The exam at a glance

  • Questions: 80.
  • Time: 60 minutes.
  • Passing score: 85%.
  • Question types: multiple choice, multiple answer, true/false.
  • Cost: $200 per attempt, taken online.
  • Prerequisite: none.
  • Validity: lifetime.

What actually trips people up

One Product Owner, one Product Backlog

The Product Owner is one person, not a committee. Multiple teams on one product share one Product Backlog and one Product Owner. Options that split ownership are wrong.

Who decides what

The PO orders the Product Backlog; the Developers decide how much they take into a Sprint and how to do the work. The PO can delegate work but stays accountable.

Value over output

Questions about measuring success expect outcomes — customer satisfaction, usage, revenue — not velocity or number of features delivered.

Cancelling a Sprint

Only the Product Owner can, and only when the Sprint Goal becomes obsolete.

Rule of thumb: when unsure, pick the answer where the Product Owner maximises value and lets the Developers self-manage.

How long to study

One to three weeks. Read the Scrum Guide closely, add Scrum.org's Product Owner learning path articles, then do timed practice exams.

Common questions

Is PSPO I hard?

Moderately. The bar is 85% and many options are almost right.

PSPO I or PSM I first?

Either works. Take the one closer to your role — the knowledge overlaps heavily.

Do I need a course?

No, the assessment can be bought directly from Scrum.org.

Does PSPO I expire?

No. It's a lifetime certification.
